# Section Mapping: Income-tax Act, 1961 → Income-tax Act, 2025

> Cross-checked against the official FAQ on Interplay/Transition (faq_transition_1961_to_2025.pdf)
> and ClearTax's section-mapping guide. Covers sections most commonly asked about in personal tax queries.

## Deductions (Chapter VI-A old → Schedule XV / Part new)

| Old Act (1961)    | New Act (2025)     | Short Description                                                                                      |
| ----------------- | ------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Section 80C       | Section 123        | Deductions for life insurance, PPF, ELSS, NSC, tuition fees, home loan principal, etc. (max ₹1,50,000) |
| Section 80CCC     | Section 123        | Contribution to pension fund (merged into 123)                                                         |
| Section 80CCD(1)  | Section 124(1)     | Employee contribution to NPS (within ₹1,50,000 aggregate limit)                                        |
| Section 80CCD(1B) | Section 124(2)     | Additional NPS contribution — extra ₹50,000 deduction                                                  |
| Section 80CCD(2)  | Section 124(3)     | Employer's NPS contribution (up to 14% of salary for central govt, 10% for others)                     |
| Section 80D       | Section 126        | Medical insurance premium (self, family, parents)                                                      |
| Section 80DD      | Section 127        | Maintenance/medical treatment of disabled dependent                                                    |
| Section 80DDB     | Section 128        | Medical treatment of specified diseases                                                                |
| Section 80E       | Section 129        | Interest on education loan                                                                             |
| Section 80EEA     | (Expired/subsumed) | Additional interest on home loan for affordable housing — no equivalent, was sunset                    |
| Section 80G       | Section 131        | Donations to approved funds/charities                                                                  |
| Section 80GG      | Section 132        | Rent paid when HRA not received                                                                        |
| Section 80TTA     | Section 153(1)     | Savings account interest deduction (up to ₹10,000) — non-senior citizens                               |
| Section 80TTB     | Section 153(2)     | Interest income deduction for senior citizens (up to ₹50,000)                                          |
| Section 80U       | Section 130        | Deduction for person with disability                                                                   |

---

> **Important notes:**
>
> - The new Act 2025 renumbers and restructures substantially. Some old sections are merged into single new sections.
> - Old regime (Section 115BAC opt-out) is still available for FY 2026-27 but the new regime under Section 202 is the **default**.
> - This mapping covers the sections most relevant to personal tax queries. For corporate/business provisions, consult the full Act.
> - Tax Year concept introduced: FY 2026-27 = Tax Year 2026-27 = AY 2027-28.
